This policy explains how the Vedanthika Dental Clinic app ("the app") handles information. The app is a practice-management tool used by clinic staff.

Information the app stores

Account details (name, email, password), and clinic records that staff enter: patient names, age, sex, phone, address, medical history, appointments, case sheets, bills, payments and expenses.

How it is used

Only to run the clinic's records, appointments and billing. We do not sell data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Each signed-in account can access only its own records.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept until the clinic deletes them. To delete your account and all associated data, email [CLINIC EMAIL] and we will remove it.

Permissions

The app needs internet access only. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
